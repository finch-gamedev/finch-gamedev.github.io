11/21/2026 #meta

Welcome to my personal site. I’m finch, a game client engineer. I’ve worked in the games industry since 2017, mainly building mobile and web games in Unity and Cocos Creator.

What’s here

  • Projects: personal projects built outside work, such as Care Label Maker, a laundry care label tool, and chives, a trading workbench for Taiwan stocks. Each one comes with an overview, the stack, and notes on how it was built.
  • About: my experience and skills.
  • Blog: the page you’re reading now.

What I’ll write about

Over the years I’ve picked up a lot from game projects, and I plan to write some of it up, for example:

  • Ways to cut game load times
  • Introducing and maintaining hot updates
  • Integrating Unity with native Android and iOS features
  • Automating builds and deployment for mobile and HTML5 games

I’ll also document how my personal projects come together, including the design trade-offs and the mistakes along the way.

The site itself is built with Astro. The source lives in a private repo, and every update deploys automatically.

To get in touch, you’ll find my email and GitHub on the About page.
