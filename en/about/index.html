About

I'm finch, a game client engineer. I've worked in the games industry since 2017, mainly with Unity and Cocos Creator in C# and TypeScript, on 2D and 3D titles across HTML5, Android and iOS. I've also integrated native features in Java and Objective-C, and taken apps from an empty project all the way to release on Google Play and the App Store. My strengths are load-time optimisation and cohesive, modular design; I care about refactoring and readable code, and I'm used to working closely with designers, artists and backend engineers.
